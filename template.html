The Babylon Fire burned 107,189 acres across Bears Ears, Dark Canyon and the Monticello Ranger District in summer 2026 — Utah's first megafire in eight years, 95% contained in early August. The closure order covered the entire Natural Bridges → Elk Ridge → Monticello section of this route, plus Canyonlands' Needles District, and was written to expire 31 August 2026. Read this before you plan around it

These are climatological averages, not a forecast. They describe a typical October, and no single October is typical. One Great Basin cold front can put 12–18 inches on the high plateaus and drop temperatures 30°F below anything on this chart — Cedar Breaks and Lizard Head Pass both close in some years before Halloween. Use this to choose a sleep system, a resupply plan, and where your bail-out points are. Then check NWS point forecasts within 72 hours of every high crossing, and be willing to change the plan.

      The carries that actually decide your food plan

      Raw distance ranks these badly. A 120-mile gap ending at a supermarket you ride straight past is far easier than a 110-mile gap that dead-ends at a closed outpost and a 28-mile round-trip detour. These are scored on what actually bites: dead stops inside the gap, the detour you have to ride to reach the far end, and dry country.

        Business directory

        Names, addresses and phone numbers cross-check across multiple sources and are reasonably solid. Hours are not. Every entry shows when it was checked and carries a phone number for exactly that reason — call, don't trust.

        Water

        The least certain data on this site — read the confidence note
        How confident am I in this? Not very.

        Town taps and agency-maintained spigots are solid. Everything else on this page is inference, not observation. I have not ridden this route, I could not obtain the official GPX, and the springs and stock tanks that matter most — Poison Spring, Hog Springs, the Elk Ridge tanks — are precisely the ones I cannot verify. Bikepacking Roots' own guidance is "do not plan on every stock tank having water." Take that seriously and treat every amber and red entry below as absent until someone who was there says otherwise.
